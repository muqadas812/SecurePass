Common Cyber Attacks

🎣 Phishing

Phishing uses fake emails, messages or websites to trick users into sharing passwords or personal information.

Stay Safe: Check links carefully and don't share sensitive information with suspicious sources.

🔨 Brute Force Attack

A brute force attack tries many possible passwords until the correct password is discovered.

Stay Safe: Use long, unique passwords and enable MFA.

🔑 Credential Stuffing

Credential stuffing uses stolen usernames and passwords to try accessing other accounts.

Stay Safe: Use a different password for every account.

Multi-Factor Authentication

Why Use MFA?

Multi-Factor Authentication adds an extra layer of security to your accounts.

It can require something you know, something you have, or something you are.
